Our Services

Discover the variety of services we offer, specializing in South Indian-based event management!

Wedding Planning

We specialize in planning traditional South Indian weddings, incorporating rituals, custom decor, and vibrant celebrations. We handle everything from venue selection, mandap setup, purohit arrangements to guest logistics and catering. Each wedding is curated with authentic traditions and modern touches.

Engagements

Celebrate your special pre-wedding event with elegance—our engagement planning includes beautiful decor, rituals, and seamless coordination. We provide customized themes, stage decoration, couple entry ideas, photography, catering, and traditional engagement ceremony setups to match your vision.

Cradle Ceremony

We specialize in organizing traditional and modern cradle ceremonies, celebrating your baby's first milestones with cultural grace and joy. Our offerings include themed decorations, traditional rituals setup, photography, live music, catering, and guest coordination to ensure a warm, memorable event.

Birthday Parties

Our expert team ensures that birthday celebrations are personalized and fun, bringing the best South Indian entertainment. We handle theme planning, games, emcees, return gifts, catering, and live performances—perfect for kids, teens, and adults alike.

Catering

Delight your guests with exquisite South and North Indian cuisine, curated by our expert chefs. We offer full-service catering for all event types, with customizable menus featuring traditional Andhra, Tamil, Kerala, and North Indian dishes. From elegant buffets to live cooking counters, we ensure authentic taste and top-tier service.

Haldi Ceremony

We design vibrant Haldi ceremonies filled with color, tradition, and joy—perfect for photo-worthy memories. Our services include floral decor, turmeric application setups, music arrangements, props, and guest coordination to ensure a cheerful and well-organized function.

Mehndi Ceremony

Celebrate your Mehndi with colorful decor, traditional elements, and joyful entertainment. We offer professional mehndi artists, vibrant themes, music & dance setups, selfie booths, catering, and complete event coordination for a fun-filled day.

Receptions

Host an elegant and grand wedding reception with flawless execution and stunning design. We manage everything from stage decor, lighting, guest welcome, entertainment, catering, photography, and coordination for an unforgettable evening.
